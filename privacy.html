1. Publisher and contact

Ferry Island Living is an independent neighbourhood guide published by Marija Zolnercika, the controller of information handled by the publisher, using the brand Zolner Studio. For support, privacy questions or a request about information you have sent to the publisher, email zolnerstudio@gmail.com.

This notice explains how information is used to provide the guide, answer support requests and keep its online services working.

The publisher relies on legitimate interests to provide the features you request, respond to support and privacy enquiries, and maintain a secure, reliable service. Requests are limited to the information needed for those purposes, taking account of your privacy rights. Legal obligations may also require handling a privacy request or responding to a binding lawful demand. External providers remain responsible for their own processing under their applicable terms.

2. Information used on your device

Saved-place identifiers are stored locally. Directory searches and filters operate on the bundled directory. Day-planning group size and occasion are used locally and are not sent in the discovery request or included in shared itinerary text.

The app does not request device GPS, camera, microphone, contacts or photo-library access. It has no resident registration, advertising identifier, in-app advertising, in-app purchase or push-notification feature in this app.

Device backup and transfer behaviour depends on the operating system. Android cloud backup is disabled in the app manifest, but some manufacturers may permit device-to-device transfers. The iOS app has no explicit backup exclusion. Removing local app data does not promise deletion of operating-system backups or copies already shared elsewhere.

3. Information sent for app features

FeatureWhat is sent or received

Travel planningA destination or place you enter, route endpoints, date and time are sent to Transport for London for journey results. Station status and arrival requests use fixed public station information.
News and railThe app requests public information from its backend. Its news and rail providers receive backend requests; your device also makes the requests needed to display the results.
Network operationHosting and data providers receive request metadata, which can include IP address, requested URL, request time and technical device/network information. Providers use this information to deliver requests, maintain their services and protect against abuse, under their applicable terms and privacy practices.

The installed app bundles its interface and does not load the hosted website’s analytics scripts. Its network requests still pass through hosting infrastructure, where request metadata and traffic may be recorded under the provider’s applicable practices. The app’s own backend diagnostics are limited to provider hostname, response status and a fixed error classification; they do not intentionally log search queries or request bodies.

4. Services and external links

  • Transport for London supplies Tube and journey information. See TfL’s privacy information.
  • OpenAI’s hosting service operates the app backend. Network requests pass through its hosting infrastructure. See OpenAI’s privacy information; the applicable service terms also govern hosting operations.
  • News and rail providers supply public content through the backend. The app identifies the source of each update. The app uses GOV.UK for official local updates; its privacy notice applies when you open GOV.UK. A separate BBC link opens the BBC website. The fallback rail provider is railinfo.uk; the app labels its source and limitations.
  • Google/Gmail handles email sent to the support mailbox. Google map links contact Google after you choose to open them. The app does not perform a live ratings lookup when displaying venue cards. Wider live Google venue discovery and the Google Maps venue-photo integration are not active in this app. The app currently sends any venue-photo lookup to its own backend while upstream photo access remains disabled. If enabled later, loading a returned Google-hosted image will also connect your device to Google’s image service. No active Google photo feed is claimed. The Google Privacy Policy is incorporated by reference for Google’s processing; the related Google Maps End User Additional Terms and our terms of use also apply to Google Maps features.

Booking, venue, operator and news websites apply their own practices when you choose to open them. The app does not make a booking or process a ticket or restaurant payment on your behalf.

Support-site hosting: GitHub Pages handles requests for these support pages under GitHub’s privacy statement. These static pages contain no analytics script, external font, tracking pixel, contact form or upload feature. Service providers may process information outside the United Kingdom under the arrangements described in their applicable terms and privacy notices.

5. Sharing and support email

Share itinerary opens your phone’s share sheet only after you choose it. You select the recipient or service. The text contains dates/times, venues, addresses, return details and caveats; it excludes group size and occasion. The receiving service controls the copy you send.

An email link opens your mail app and does not send automatically. If you send a support message, the publisher receives the sender details shown by your mail app, such as your name and email address, and your message through Gmail. Marija Zolnercika and one authorised support collaborator may read that mailbox solely to handle support and privacy requests. Marija handles privacy requests. There is no resident account to register or delete.

6. Retention and security

The app does not create a resident profile database. Local saved places remain until removed or local app data is cleared. Planning preferences and results are held in session memory rather than a resident account.

The backend’s short-lived news and rail caches contain public content rather than resident profiles. Request processing and provider logs are separate from those content caches. Application requests are designed to use HTTPS.

Resolved support messages are deleted six months after resolution. You can ask Marija about access, correction or deletion using the support address. Gmail’s independent retention and recovery processes follow Google’s applicable policies.

Service providers retain operational information for their service delivery, security, legal and other stated purposes under their applicable terms and privacy notices. Those provider records are separate from the publisher’s support mailbox and from the app’s short-lived public-content caches.

7. Your choices and questions

You can use the directory without an account, remove a bookmark in Saved places, choose not to send an email and choose not to share an itinerary or open an external site. Travel and network-dependent planning features need internet access.

For questions about a support message or information held by the publisher, contact zolnerstudio@gmail.com. Requests relating to information held independently by a provider may also need to be directed to that provider. Depending on the applicable legal conditions, you may request access to a copy of your information, correction, deletion, restriction of processing or data portability. You may object to processing based on legitimate interests. Marija handles these requests for information held by the publisher and will explain any applicable limits. You can also raise a concern with the UK Information Commissioner’s Office.
