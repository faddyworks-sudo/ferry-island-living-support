2. Local information and day plans

Venue descriptions, opening information and events come from identified sources and can change. Transport feeds may be delayed, limited or unavailable. Scheduled times are distinct from supplied estimates. Check important journey, admission and accessibility details with the relevant operator or venue before travelling.

A day plan suggests activities, meals and routes. It does not reserve tickets or tables, confirm group capacity or guarantee admission. A historical venue photograph does not establish the venue’s current appearance or condition.
