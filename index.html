Common questions

Using the guide

Why are some travel updates unavailable?

Transport information depends on external providers. The app labels unavailable or limited feeds and keeps scheduled rail times separate from supplied estimates. Check the operator’s official information before travelling.

How do I remove a saved place?

Open More, then Your saved places, and tap the place’s bookmark to remove it. Saved places are stored on the device rather than in a resident account.

Does a day plan make bookings for me?

No. A plan suggests visits, meals and routes. It does not reserve tables or tickets, confirm group capacity or guarantee admission. Follow a venue’s booking or ticket link when needed.

Can I contact the building’s management here?

This is an independent local guide, not a building-management or emergency service. Use the contact details supplied by your property manager for building matters.
